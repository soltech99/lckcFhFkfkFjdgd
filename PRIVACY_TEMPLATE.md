# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction
This application is an internal productivity tool for authorized enterprise 
employees to automate workflows using Google Workspace APIs.

## 2. Google Data We Access

We access the following Google user data with your explicit permission:

### Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.send)
- **What we access**: Your Gmail messages
- **Purpose**: To extract structured data (invoices, receipts) for automated 
  processing and to send email notifications on your behalf
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events)
- **What we access**: Your calendar events
- **Purpose**: To display events in the task board and create automated reminders
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Google Docs (documents.readonly)
- **What we access**: Your Google Docs documents
- **Purpose**: To extract content for workflow automation
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Google Sheets (spreadsheets)
- **What we access**: Your spreadsheet data
- **Purpose**: To execute SQL-like queries and generate automated reports
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Google Slides (presentations.readonly)
- **What we access**: Your presentations
- **Purpose**: To extract content for reporting purposes
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Google Drive (drive.readonly)
- **What we access**: Your Drive files
- **Purpose**: To access and execute custom Google Apps Scripts
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Apps Script (script.external_request)
- **What we access**: Your custom Google Apps Scripts
- **Purpose**: To execute user-created automation workflows
- **Storage**: OAuth tokens only (encrypted)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your Google data ONLY for:
- Automating your workflow tasks
- Executing custom scripts you create
- Generating reports based on your data
- Sending emails on your behalf

We DO NOT:
- ❌ Share your data with third parties
- ❌ Sell your data
- ❌ Use your data for advertising
- ❌ Store your Google data on our servers (except encrypted OAuth tokens)

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **OAuth Tokens**: Encrypted and stored locally on your device
- **Google Data**: Not stored (accessed only when needed via API)
- **Communications**: All API calls use HTTPS encryption
- **Access Control**: Only you can access your data

## 5. Data Retention

- **OAuth Tokens**: Retained until you revoke access
- **Application Data**: Stored only on your local device
- **Google Data**: Not retained

## 6. Your Rights and Controls

You can:
- ✅ View your connected Google account: Settings → Account
- ✅ Revoke access anytime: Settings → Disconnect Google
- ✅ Delete all local data: Settings → Delete Account
- ✅ Revoke access via Google: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## 7. Data Deletion

To delete your data:
1. Open the application
2. Go to Settings → Account
3. Click "Disconnect Google" to revoke OAuth access
4. Click "Delete All Data" to remove local data

Or revoke access directly from your Google Account:
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## 8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Google APIs**: For accessing your Google Workspace data

We do not share your Google data with any third-party services.
